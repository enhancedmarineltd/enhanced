About Enhanced Marine

Built for the Blue.

We are a marine inspection and subsea services company focused on providing reliable underwater visibility for critical marine assets and offshore operations.

Who We Are

Experience beneath the surface. Clarity above it.

Enhanced Marine Limited provides marine inspections, ROV operations, mooring and riser inspections, and chain dimensional measurement using a specialized tool.

Our work is centred around helping clients understand the condition of their underwater assets through structured inspection and reliable visual information.

We understand that subsea environments can be complex and difficult to access. Our approach is therefore focused on combining marine expertise, technology and disciplined inspection processes.
